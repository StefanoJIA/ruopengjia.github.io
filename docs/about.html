Profile / Ruopeng Jia

About Me

My path through electrical engineering, photonics, and scientific instrumentation.

Who I Am

I’m an electrical engineer interested in the full path from a physical signal to a useful measurement. Astronomy first drew me to questions about what instruments can detect. My graduate work brought me closer to the device itself, through the design of coupled optical resonators and their spectral response.

Education

  • Northeastern University, Boston, MA
    Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering, 2024
    Specialized in Electromagnetics, Plasma, and Optics. My coursework included Nanophotonics, Quantum Engineering, and Electromagnetic Theories.
  • Northern Arizona University, Flagstaff, AZ
    Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering, 2022
    Gained expertise in Semiconductor Memories, Microprocessors, and Electromagnetic Fundamentals, completing a capstone project on automated greenhouse climate control systems.

Current Focus

My current interests center on designing photonic systems for scientific measurement:

  • Integrated photonics: Coupled resonators, programmable spectral responses, and compact optical systems.
  • Optical measurement: Photon counting, calibration, background characterization, and precision instrumentation.
  • Scientific inference: Weak-signal analysis, quantization, and robust hardware implementation.

Skills

  • Programming Languages: Python, C++, MATLAB, R, SQL
  • Signal processing: GWPy, Bilby, and AMPLFI for waveform analysis and inference.
  • Optics: Resonator modeling, spectral engineering, photon counting, and optical alignment.
  • Machine learning and hardware: Quantized classifiers, hls4ml, and FPGA validation.

Professional Experience

Graduate research, Northeastern University (2023–2024)
Studied coupled resonator arrays and methods to control their optical spectra.

Junior Research Associate, QuantumCTek, Jinan, China (2023)
Helped align and calibrate a photon-counting optical link and characterize its field performance.

Technical Specialist, IntelliOpus Engineering (2024–present)
Work on product setup, functional validation, and translating field issues into reproducible tests.

Co-founder, Long Voyage (2026–present)
Helped design trip coordination, dispatch, and service workflows for passenger transportation.

Teaching

At Northeastern, I supported electrical engineering laboratory and electromagnetics instruction. I also teach mathematics and engineering through worked examples and structured problem sets.

What I Want to Build

I want to build instruments that extract more information from limited light. That means thinking about device response, detector noise, calibration, and the available computation together. I am especially interested in ideas that can be tested at the device or system level.

Beyond the Lab

I read about social science and history, play soccer, practice Chinese calligraphy, and listen to classical music. My gallery collects a few books and recordings that have stayed with me.
