About Ruopeng Jia

I’m an electrical engineer interested in the path from a physical signal to a useful measurement. Astronomy first drew me to weak signals; graduate work in photonics brought me closer to the devices that make those measurements possible.

Research interests

  • Integrated photonics: Coupled resonators, spectral response, and compact optical systems.
  • Optical measurement: Photon counting, calibration, and background characterization.
  • Scientific inference: Weak-signal analysis, quantization, and hardware validation.

I want to design instruments that extract more information from limited light by considering device response, detector noise, calibration, and computation together.
