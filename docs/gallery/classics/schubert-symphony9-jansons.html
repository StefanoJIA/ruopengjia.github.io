Schubert: Symphony No. 9

Mariss Jansons

Long lines, changing colors.

Follow a musical phrase beyond its first appearance. Notice what changes when it returns in a different register or orchestral color.

  • Listen for the balance between the moving line and its accompaniment.
  • On a second listen, follow one instrumental group rather than the whole orchestra.

A listening prompt, rather than a ranking: the aim is to leave room for a closer listen and a different interpretation.
