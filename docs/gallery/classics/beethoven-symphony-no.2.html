Beethoven: Symphony No. 2

Claudio Abbado

Momentum and breathing room.

Listen for the relationship between forward motion and the spaces around it: a pause, a softer response, or a change in articulation.

  • Follow how short rhythmic ideas pass between instrumental groups.
  • On a second listen, notice how the same passage feels when you focus on the bass line.

A listening prompt, rather than a ranking: the aim is to leave room for a closer listen and a different interpretation.
