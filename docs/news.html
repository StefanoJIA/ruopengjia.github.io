October 2026Research

Taking a waveform classifier onto the board

My independent study with public CUORE waveforms reached physical FPGA validation. All 1,350 validation scores matched the fixed-point HLS C reference. The follow-up comparison also showed why core timing, transport, and observation time need to be reported separately.
